This Week in Christian Books (October 20, 2026)

🗂️ SPIRITUAL DEPTH & CHRISTIAN GROWTH | ⏱️ 3-Min Microlearning 📌 STACK: The End of the Sacred-Secular Divide

📖 CARD 1: CONTEXT & ORIGIN > "Did you know that for centuries, the church has accidentally created a 'two-tier' system of life, where prayer is holy but your job is neutral, and your body is a temple but your hobbies are distractions?" • Source & Author: Jeremy Treat, a pastor and author known for bridging theology with everyday life, published by IVP (InterVarsity Press). • Genesis: Written to dismantle the harmful "sacred-secular" divide that leaves many believers feeling compartmentalized. It addresses the modern crisis where Christians struggle to see how the Gospel applies to their work, hobbies, meals, and mental health, rather than just their Sunday routine.

💡 CARD 2: THE CORE EPIPHANY > "The gospel is not meant to make us hyperspiritual; it is meant to make us fully human." • Why it matters: This flips the script on "spiritual" performance. It reveals that disconnecting faith from your daily grind isn't a sign of maturity, but a failure to trust God with the ordinary. If God isn't interested in your Tuesday afternoon meeting, He isn't interested in your Tuesday morning prayer.

🛠️ CARD 3: THE TACTICAL PRINCIPLE • Key Concept: Total Stewardship – The belief that every sphere of existence (work, leisure, body, mind) is a venue for worship, not just the church building. • Execution: * Reframe Work: Before a meeting, stop praying for "success" and start praying for "integrity and excellence as an act of worship to the King." * Sanctify Leisure: When you pick up a hobby or a book, consciously view it as a gift from God to enjoy, not a "waste of time" that steals from your spiritual duties. * Integrate Body & Soul: Acknowledge that physical health and mental peace are not "secular" issues to be ignored, but part of the holistic human design God cares about.

🎯 CARD 4: THE MICRO-CHALLENGE • Today's Action: Identify one "secular" task you are doing right now (e.g., answering emails, cooking dinner, driving). Pause for 10 seconds. Silently say: "God, I do this not just to get it done, but to honor You with my attention and care." Do this once for work, once for a hobby, and once for a relationship.

💬 CARD 5: REFLECT & INTERNALIZE • Daily Question: "Where in my life am I treating God as a Sunday guest rather than a Tuesday roommate?"
