What are the Warning Signs of Childhood Suicide?

🗂️ FAMILY & INTENTIONAL FATHERHOOD | ⏱️ 3‑Min Microlearning 📌 STACK: “When Silence Screams: Spotting the Hidden Cry of a Child”

---

📖 CARD 1: CONTEXT & ORIGIN > Did you know that more than three‑quarters of parents never realize their child has entertained suicide?

• Source & Author: Article “What are the Warning Signs of Childhood Suicide?” – parenting‑focused health site (expert‑curated, faith‑centered). • Genesis: Written to confront a growing crisis—child suicide now ranks 8th among deaths for ages 5‑10 and 2nd for ages 10‑19. The author warns parents that ignorance, not lack of love, fuels the tragedy.

---

💡 CARD 2: THE CORE EPIPHANY > “A child’s warning signs are louder than their silence—if you hear even one, you must act.”

• Why it matters: Most attempts are preceded by clear signals. Ignoring them is the fastest route to regret; responding saves lives.

---

🛠️ CARD 3: THE TACTICAL PRINCIPLE • Key Concept: Shepherd‑Care Guardrails – Jesus calls us “the good shepherd” (John 10:11); a shepherd watches, intervenes, and removes danger. • Execution: 1. Observe & Log – Each day, note any shift (sleep, appetite, mood, withdrawal, talk of death). Keep a simple checklist on your phone. 2. Ask Directly – In a low‑pressure setting (walk, car ride), pose one open‑ended question: “I’ve noticed you’ve been… What’s on your mind?” 3. Secure the Means – If a lethal tool is accessible (meds, firearms, knives), move it out of reach immediately and lock it away.

---

🎯 CARD 4: THE MICRO‑CHALLENGE • Today's Action: Spend 3 minutes scanning your child’s recent behavior for any listed warning sign. If you spot one, schedule a 5‑minute walk‑and‑talk right now, using the question template above.

---

💬 CARD 5: REFLECT & INTERNALIZE • Daily Question: “What silent alarm am I hearing today, and how will I answer it before it turns into a scream?”
