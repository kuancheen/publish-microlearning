Tips for Resolving Conflict in Marriage

🗂️ SPIRITUAL DEPTH & CHRISTIAN GROWTH | ⏱️ 3-Min Microlearning 📌 STACK: The 10-to-1 Rule & The Grace of Light

📖 CARD 1: CONTEXT & ORIGIN > "Did you know that most marital conflict isn't caused by a lack of love, but by a lack of 'conflict literacy'—the specific, learned skill of how to disagree without destroying intimacy?" • Source & Author: Aileen and the author (a pastor and counselor associated with Challies), drawing on the wisdom of Robert Murray M’Cheyne and Ephesians 4. • Genesis: Written in response to a common pastoral observation: many couples have never been taught how to resolve conflict. They rely on inherited, broken patterns (shouting, stonewalling, or texting) rather than intentional, grace-filled engagement.

💡 CARD 2: THE CORE EPIPHANY > "Your first calling before the Lord is to deal with your own sin so you can put it to death and come alive to righteousness." • Why it matters: We are experts in our spouse’s faults but amateurs in our own. Shifting the focus from "fixing them" to "submitting to scrutiny" transforms conflict from a battle of wills into a shared journey of holiness.

🛠️ CARD 3: THE TACTICAL PRINCIPLE • Key Concept: The 10-to-1 Ratio & The Light of Day • Execution: * The 10-to-1 Ratio: For every one specific sin you identify in your spouse, identify ten in yourself. This prevents the "expert on their faults" mindset. * The 10 PM Rule: If a conflict isn’t resolved by 10 PM, call a halt. Kiss, pray, affirm love, and sleep. Emotion-driven arguments at night rarely end well; let the sun rise on a fresh, reasoned perspective. * Get It in the Light: If you are stuck, invite a trusted friend or elder to sit with you. You don’t need them to mediate; their presence alone prevents emotional acting out and keeps sin from thriving in the dark.

🎯 CARD 4: THE MICRO-CHALLENGE • Today's Action: The "Safe Setting" Invitation. Text your spouse: "I value your honesty and my own growth. Can we set aside 10 minutes this week where you can share one area where you feel I’ve sinned or fallen short? I promise to listen without defending or reacting. I just want to hear it."* Note: Do not do this in the heat of an argument. Do it when calm.*

💬 CARD 5: REFLECT & INTERNALIZE • Daily Question: "Am I keeping a scorecard of my spouse’s failures, or am I actively searching for the evidences of God’s grace working in their life?"
