The Trump Administration Has Resettled Zero Religious Refugees This Year

🗂️ SPIRITUAL DEPTH & CHRISTIAN GROWTH | ⏱️ 3-Min Microlearning 📌 STACK: The Cost of Silence: When Comfort Replaces Compassion

📖 CARD 1: CONTEXT & ORIGIN > "Did you know that while over 4,800 Christians were killed for their faith last year, the United States is on track to admit zero religious refugees in 2026?" • Source & Author: Report by World Relief and Open Doors US, published in Christianity Today. • Genesis: This report highlights a stark disconnect between the global reality of the persecuted church and US policy. It exposes how political rhetoric often masks administrative inaction, leaving believers in high-risk countries without the historic safety net of American asylum.

💡 CARD 2: THE CORE EPIPHANY > "True faithfulness is not measured by what we say about the persecuted, but by what we do for them when it is inconvenient." • Why it matters: We often confuse vocal support with tangible sacrifice. When we allow our comfort to dictate our compassion, we fail the biblical mandate of hospitality, effectively closing the door on those fleeing for their lives.

🛠️ CARD 3: THE TACTICAL PRINCIPLE • Key Concept: Active Solidarity (Romans 12:15 - "Rejoice with those who rejoice; mourn with those who mourn.") • Execution: * Pray with Specificity: Move beyond generic "prayer for the persecuted" to naming specific countries (e.g., Iran, North Korea, China) and specific families in your prayers. * Advocate Locally: Contact your congressional representatives not just to complain, but to demand data and policy changes regarding refugee resettlement caps. * Sponsor a Story: Identify one refugee family or ministry (like World Relief or Open Doors) and commit to a monthly financial or volunteer contribution to sustain their work.

🎯 CARD 4: THE MICRO-CHALLENGE • Today's Action: Spend 3 minutes writing a specific prayer for one Christian family currently facing persecution in a top-50 Open Doors country. Include their names if known, or their specific location. Then, send a text to one trusted friend or church leader sharing one fact from this report to spark a conversation about how your local church can respond.

💬 CARD 5: REFLECT & INTERNALIZE • Daily Question: "Where in my life am I prioritizing my comfort over the urgent needs of my brothers and sisters in Christ?"
