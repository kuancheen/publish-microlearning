Works & Wonders (November 1)

🗂️ SPIRITUAL DEPTH & CHRISTIAN GROWTH | ⏱️ 3-Min Microlearning 📌 STACK: The Unfathomable Comfort of Omnipotent Goodness

📖 CARD 1: CONTEXT & ORIGIN > "Did you know that the theological attribute of God’s 'Power' is actually terrifying in isolation? Without the simultaneous presence of His goodness, His omnipotence would mean there is nothing to stop Him from destroying you instantly." • Source & Author: Jen Wilkin, a prominent theologian and author known for her work on the doctrines of God, quoted within the Works & Wonders devotional column (Nov 1). • Genesis: This insight addresses a common spiritual anxiety: the fear of a capricious or tyrannical deity. It clarifies that God’s character is indivisible; He cannot act in power without also acting in wisdom, justice, and love.

💡 CARD 2: THE CORE EPIPHANY > "God’s power is not a threat to be feared, but a guarantee of safety, because it is inextricably bound to His limitless goodness." • Why it matters: We often view God’s power as separate from His character—like a force of nature. This insight reframes power as the vehicle through which His goodness is delivered. It shifts your mindset from "What if He is angry?" to "He is all-powerful, and He is all-good, so I am secure."

🛠️ CARD 3: THE TACTICAL PRINCIPLE • Key Concept: The Indivisibility of Divine Attributes (The "Power + Goodness" Equation). • Execution: * In Anxiety: When facing a crisis (business loss, health scare), stop praying for "control" and start praying for "trust." Remind yourself: "His power is too great to fail, and His goodness is too pure to harm me." * In Fatherhood: Model this to your children. When they are afraid of the dark or a storm, don't just say "It's okay." Say, "God is bigger than this storm, and He loves you more than this fear." In Prayer: Shift from petitioning God to change the situation to praising Him for who He is in* the situation. "Lord, I trust Your power to handle this, and I trust Your goodness to make it right."

🎯 CARD 4: THE MICRO-CHALLENGE • Today's Action: The "Attribute Anchor." Pick one specific worry you are carrying today. Write down two attributes of God that counter that worry (e.g., Worry: "I will fail" → Attributes: "His Power sustains me" + "His Goodness forgives me"). Read this pair aloud three times before your next meeting or drive.

💬 CARD 5: REFLECT & INTERNALIZE • Daily Question: "Am I treating God’s power as a weapon I need to defend against, or as a shield I can rest in?"
