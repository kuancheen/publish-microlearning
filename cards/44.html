10 Things You Need To Know About Bullying and Cyberbullying

🗂️ FAMILY & INTENTIONAL FATHERHOOD | ⏱️ 3‑Min Microlearning 📌 STACK: “Spot the Silent Threat – A Father’s 5‑Step Bullying Guard”

---

### 📖 CARD 1: CONTEXT & ORIGIN > “Did you know that 1 in 5 kids says they’re bullied, and 1 in 6 says they’re cyber‑bullied?”

- Source & Author: Focus on the Family editorial team – a trusted Christian parenting platform that aggregates CDC and NCES data. - Genesis: Written to equip parents with a concise, faith‑grounded briefing on modern bullying—both hallway and screen‑based—so fathers can intervene before the pain becomes lifelong.

---

### 💡 CARD 2: THE CORE EPIPHANY > “Bullying isn’t a phase; it’s a hidden epidemic that steals a child’s dignity and future.”

- Why it matters: When a child’s worth is attacked, the wound follows them into adulthood—affecting confidence, relationships, and even spiritual health. Recognizing the scope turns “it’s just kids being kids” into a call for intentional protection.

---

### 🛠️ CARD 3: THE TACTICAL PRINCIPLE - Key Concept: “Guardianship of the Image” – every child reflects God’s image; protecting that image is a biblical mandate (Genesis 1:27; Mark 12:31). - Execution: 1. Daily Scan: Spend 2 minutes each evening reviewing your child’s digital inbox, social feeds, and school messages for any red flags (harsh words, exclusion posts, unknown contacts). 2. Conversation Anchor: Ask a simple, open‑ended question at dinner: “Did anyone say something that hurt you today, online or offline?” Listen without judgment. 3. Model Response: When a hurtful incident is shared, respond with Christ‑like love: “I’m sorry you experienced that. Let’s pray together and plan how we’ll stand up for you.”

---

### 🎯 CARD 4: THE MICRO‑CHALLENGE - Today's Action: Set a 3‑minute “Digital Doorway” alarm on your phone for 7 PM. When it rings, open your child’s most‑used app (with their permission) and glance for any negative messages or posts. If you spot anything, note it and schedule a 5‑minute talk tomorrow morning.

---

### 💬 CARD 5: REFLECT & INTERNALIZE - Daily Question: “If I were the one being targeted, would I feel loved and protected by my father today?”

Carry this question through your day; let it shape every interaction with your children and guide you to act as the steadfast shield God calls you to be.
