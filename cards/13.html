The Trump Administration Has Resettled Zero Religious Refugees This Year

🗂️ SPIRITUAL DEPTH & CHRISTIAN GROWTH | ⏱️ 3-Min Microlearning 📌 STACK: The Gap Between Pulpit & Policy

📖 CARD 1: CONTEXT & ORIGIN • Source & Author: Report by World Relief and Open Doors US, published in Christianity Today. These are leading Christian humanitarian organizations tracking global persecution. • Background & Genesis: This analysis addresses a stark 2026 reality: despite record-high numbers of Christians being killed or forced to flee for their faith (4,849 killed; 224,000+ displaced), the U.S. refugee program has admitted zero religious refugees this fiscal year. The article highlights the dissonance between the American evangelical identity as a "nation of refuge" and the current administrative retreat from that historic role, including the detention of already-resettled Christian families.

💡 CARD 2: THE CORE EPIPHANY > "Faith without advocacy is merely sentiment; if we claim to be a people of mercy, we must ensure the door remains open for those fleeing persecution." • Why it matters: There is a dangerous disconnect between our private devotion and our public posture. When 70-82% of evangelicals believe the U.S. has a moral responsibility to welcome refugees, but policy reflects the opposite, it signals a failure of spiritual stewardship. We are not just passive observers of global suffering; we are responsible for the integrity of our witness.

🛠️ CARD 3: THE TACTICAL PRINCIPLE • Key Concept: Prophetic Accountability (Amos 5:24 - "Let justice roll on like a river") • Execution: * Audit Your Assumptions: Stop assuming that "supporting Christian leaders" automatically means the persecuted church is being protected. Verify the facts. * Move from Prayer to Petition: Prayer is essential, but it must be paired with civic engagement. Understand the current refugee ceiling and the specific barriers preventing admission. * Speak to Your Community: In your church or small group, bring up the specific data (zero admissions) to break the silence. Silence complicity is a spiritual hazard.

🎯 CARD 4: THE MICRO-CHALLENGE • Today's Action: The 3-Minute Fact Check. 1. Open your browser and look up the current U.S. refugee admission ceiling for Fiscal Year 2026. 2. Write down one specific name of a persecuted Christian group mentioned in the report (e.g., Karen Christians in Myanmar, Iranian Christians). 3. Send a text to one trusted friend or church leader with this single sentence: "Did you know the U.S. has admitted zero religious refugees this year? Here is the link to the report."

💬 CARD 5: REFLECT & INTERNALIZE • Daily Question: "If I were a Christian in Iran or Myanmar today, would I feel that the American church is my ally, or my bystander? What is one concrete step I can take this week to ensure I am an ally?"
