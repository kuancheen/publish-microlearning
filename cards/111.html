Seeing Motherhood Through Eternal Eyes

💡 Core Takeaway

A grand view of God’s glory transforms the daily reality of raising children.

Gloria Furman argues that when your eyes are fixed on the horizon of eternity, it fundamentally affects your vision for motherhood. This perspective shifts parenting from a task of mere management to a participation in God’s grand narrative. In a culture that often reduces motherhood to logistics or self-fulfillment, Furman’s insight offers a spiritual anchor that keeps the heart steady.

The core principle is that our view of God must be big and glorious enough to transform our perspective of the mundane. When we see the sheer majesty of the Creator, the small struggles of daily life—messy rooms, difficult conversations, and fleeting moments of peace—gain a new weight and significance. It is not about ignoring the hard parts, but about seeing them within a larger, redemptive story that points toward eternity.

In real life, this means approaching parenting with a sense of awe rather than just anxiety. It changes how we pray, how we handle frustration, and how we cherish the ordinary. When the horizon is wide, the immediate obstacles do not define the whole picture.

Try it today: Spend five minutes in prayer before your next parenting challenge. Ask God to show you one aspect of your child’s life that points to His eternal glory, and hold that truth in your mind as you interact with them.
🤔 How does your view of God’s greatness change the way you see your daily responsibilities?

Source: Gloria Furman, via Challies, A La Carte (November 11)
