I Regret to Inform You That Heaven Tourism Is Back

🗂️ SPIRITUAL DEPTH & CHRISTIAN GROWTH | ⏱️ 3-Min Microlearning 📌 STACK: The Danger of "Heaven Tourism"

📖 CARD 1: CONTEXT & ORIGIN > "Did you know that a genre of Christian books called 'Heaven Tourism'—where authors claim to have left their bodies to visit the afterlife—has returned to bestseller lists, despite being widely criticized by theologians for mixing truth with dangerous speculation?" • Source & Author: This analysis is by John MacArthur (a renowned pastor, theologian, and author of The MacArthur Study Bible), writing for Challies.com. He is reviewing Gabe Poirot’s 18 Days in Heaven, a recent bestseller. • Genesis: MacArthur writes this in response to the resurgence of "near-death experience" (NDE) books like Don Piper’s 90 Minutes in Heaven and Todd Burpo’s Heaven Is for Real. He argues that while these books contain emotional appeal, they often introduce theological errors that contradict Scripture, creating a "tourist" view of heaven rather than a biblically grounded one.

💡 CARD 2: THE CORE EPIPHANY > "If your theology of God is based on a 18-day coma dream rather than the written Word, you are building your house on sand, not rock." • Why it matters: NDE books often present a God who is "relationally incomplete" (needing humans to fill a lack) or who "forfeits sovereignty" to allow evil. This contradicts the biblical God who is self-sufficient, sovereign, and the ultimate authority. Relying on subjective, unverifiable experiences over objective Scripture leads to a distorted view of who God actually is.

🛠️ CARD 3: THE TACTICAL PRINCIPLE • Key Concept: Sola Scriptura as the Litmus Test • Execution: * The "Weighting" Rule: When you encounter a powerful spiritual testimony, book, or sermon, immediately ask: "Does this align with the clear teaching of Scripture?" If it adds "new revelation" not found in the Bible, treat it with extreme caution. * Identify the "God" Being Described: Check if the source portrays God as needy, surprised, or limited. The biblical God is never surprised, never lacks, and is never bound by human free will in a way that compromises His sovereignty. * Prioritize the Written Word: Use the Bible as the final arbiter of truth. If an experience contradicts Scripture, the experience is invalid, not the Scripture.

🎯 CARD 4: THE MICRO-CHALLENGE • Today's Action: The 3-Minute Scripture Audit. 1. Pick one popular Christian book, podcast, or social media post you’ve consumed recently that felt "spiritually deep." 2. Identify one specific claim or "insight" it presented. 3. Open your Bible (or a reliable app) and search for that concept. 4. If you cannot find clear biblical support for that specific claim, mark it as "Speculation" in your mind. Do not let it shape your core theology.

💬 CARD 5: REFLECT & INTERNALIZE • Daily Question: "Am I letting my emotions or 'experiences' define who God is, or am I letting the clear, unchanging Word of God define my experience?"
