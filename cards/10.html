The Trump Administration Has Resettled Zero Religious Refugees This Year

🗂️ SPIRITUAL DEPTH & CHRISTIAN GROWTH | ⏱️ 3-Min Microlearning 📌 STACK: The Danger of Theological Comfort Without Political Action

📖 CARD 1: CONTEXT & ORIGIN • Source & Author: Reported by Christianity Today, citing data from World Relief and Open Doors US. Key voices include Pastor Ara Torosian (Farsi-speaking congregation in LA) and Myal Greene (CEO of World Relief). • Background & Genesis: This report highlights a stark contradiction in 2026: While 4,849 Christians were killed for their faith globally, the US admitted zero religious refugees this fiscal year. The administration prioritized non-persecuted groups, leading to the detention of existing refugee families (including children) under "Operation PARRIS." This exposes a gap between evangelical political alignment and the tangible reality of the persecuted church.

💡 CARD 2: THE CORE EPIPHANY > "When the door closes on the persecuted, the church’s witness becomes a hollow echo of its own theology." • Why it matters: 70% of evangelicals believe the US has a moral duty to welcome refugees, yet policy has moved in the opposite direction. This creates a crisis of credibility: if we claim to love the "least of these" (Matthew 25) but support policies that leave them in danger, our spiritual authority erodes. Comfortable faith without compassionate action is spiritually bankrupt.

🛠️ CARD 3: THE TACTICAL PRINCIPLE • Key Concept: Prophetic Accountability – The duty to align political engagement with biblical mandates for justice and mercy, rather than tribal loyalty. • Execution: * Audit Your Assumptions: Stop assuming that "pro-family" or "pro-Christian" political labels automatically equate to pro-justice outcomes. Verify the data behind the policies you support. * Shift from Voting to Advocacy: Move beyond the ballot box. Identify one specific humanitarian organization (like World Relief or Open Doors) and engage with their policy briefs to understand the legislative levers that keep doors open. * Speak the Truth in Love: In church or small groups, gently but firmly name the discrepancy between our stated values (mercy) and our political silence or support for restrictive policies.

🎯 CARD 4: THE MICRO-CHALLENGE • Today's Action: Spend 3 minutes looking up the current US refugee ceiling for the next fiscal year. Then, write down one specific, polite question you can ask a trusted elected representative or local leader about how they intend to uphold the US commitment to religious freedom for the persecuted church.

💬 CARD 5: REFLECT & INTERNALIZE • Daily Question: "Am I confusing political tribalism with Christian stewardship, and what is the cost to my integrity if I stay silent on the plight of the persecuted?"
