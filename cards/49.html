🗂️ PERSONAL MASTERY & PROFESSIONAL EDGE | ⏱️ 3-Min Microlearning 📌 STACK: The Wooden System: Concentrate Power Where It Compounds

📖 CARD 1: CONTEXT & ORIGIN > "Did you know that the most dominant basketball coach in history, John Wooden, didn’t just coach hard—he engineered a system where 100% of the playing time went to his top 7 players, creating a feedback loop of excellence that no other team could match?" • Source & Author: Charlie Munger (1924–2023), Vice Chairman of Berkshire Hathaway and the "wisdom" partner to Warren Buffett. • Genesis: This insight comes from a previously unreleased 2022 conversation between Munger and Todd Combs (Berkshire’s investment manager). Munger uses the "Wooden Lesson" to explain why Berkshire and companies like Teledyne (under Henry Singleton) achieved such outsized success: they didn’t spread resources thin; they identified the few who were "geniuses" and gave them the power to dominate their fields.

💡 CARD 2: THE CORE EPIPHANY > "Average effort spread across many tasks yields average results; concentrated power in the hands of the few who are truly exceptional yields exponential returns." • Why it matters: Most leaders (and fathers) suffer from "dilution syndrome"—trying to be good at everything or giving equal attention to all opportunities/people. Munger argues that true mastery comes from recognizing that some people and problems are "size 16 shoes with webbed feet" (unfairly advantaged) and that you must bet big on them, ignoring the conventional wisdom of "fairness" or "balance."

🛠️ CARD 3: THE TACTICAL PRINCIPLE • Key Concept: The Wooden System (Concentrated Delegation & Focus) • Execution: * In Business: Identify your top 10% of clients or team members who drive 80% of your value. Stop trying to "fix" the bottom 50%. Give the top tier more autonomy, resources, and trust. If they are "mini-Woodens," let them play. * In Fatherhood: You cannot be a "good enough" dad to all your children simultaneously if you are spreading your energy thin. Identify the specific, high-leverage moments (e.g., the 15 minutes of undivided attention before bed) and protect them fiercely. Concentrate your emotional capital there. * In Personal Growth: Stop collecting "cigar butts" (small, low-value habits). Pick the one skill or habit that compounds (e.g., deep reading, strategic thinking) and give it 100% of your prime focus time.

🎯 CARD 4: THE MICRO-CHALLENGE • Today's Action: Perform a "Wooden Audit" of your calendar or to-do list. Identify the one task or relationship that yields the highest return on your time and energy. Delete or delegate the two lowest-value items that are currently draining you. Spend the next 5 minutes planning how to give that high-value item your full, undivided attention tomorrow.

💬 CARD 5: REFLECT & INTERNALIZE • Daily Question: "Where am I trying to be 'fair' instead of 'effective'? Who or what in my life is my 'top 7,' and am I giving them the power to win?"
